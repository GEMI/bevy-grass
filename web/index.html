bevy_grass

Grass for Bevy, drawn by the GPU in your browser through WebGPU.

Controls WASD to fly, Q and E down and up, Shift faster. Hold the right mouse button to look around, or press M to keep looking. Hold the left mouse button to paint with the brush. 1 to 4 pick the quality, [ and ] the preset.

Every public setting of the crate, read from its types. Click a name for its documentation. Changes are kept in the address, so the link shares them.
